About Rand

Rand Smedley grew up in Florida and at eighteen landed his first real job as a stuntman at Six Gun Territory, an Old West theme park, where he played a young deputy and died in gunfights several times a day. At twenty, he traded his six-gun for a sales career that spanned decades of selling, managing, and training.

In 2012, he became a flight instructor, logging more than 4,000 hours teaching in single- and multi-engine aircraft. He ran his own flight school in San Diego, flying often over the deserts around Yuma and Tucson that would one day become the setting for Mac McAllister’s encounter with the crew of the USS Vanguard.

After retiring from flying in 2020, Rand turned to writing. The Geronimo Solution is his first novel. Today he writes full-time in Arizona, where he lives with his wife and three cats.
