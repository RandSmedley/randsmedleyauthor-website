About Rand

Rand Smedley writes science fiction with its boots in the dust of the American West.

He grew up in the American South and spent a long career in sales, sales management, and sales training, a line of work where reading what people don’t say and telling a good story are the whole job.

In 2010 he left sales, earned his commercial pilot’s license, and became a flight instructor, eventually opening his own flight training center in Southern California. Many of his former students now fly for major airlines. He retired from instructing in 2020, and The Geronimo Solution grew out of the quiet that followed.

Just a few hours’ drive from where Mac McAllister panned for gold, Rand lives in Arizona with his wife and three cats.

His aim is genre fiction with fully realized characters: people with complicated motives, real histories, and shifting loyalties.
