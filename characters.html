Characters

A Note from Rand Smedley

Thanks again for spending your time in the Geronimo universe.

Each of us, writer and reader, creates in our minds images that bring the story to life, such as Mac’s battered hat, Harry Hennessey’s girth and his ever-present cigar, and Tamyra Corbett’s short blonde hair and rigid bearing.

That is the fun of reading and the joy of writing stories with rich characters and settings. Each of us “sees” variations of the character, filtered through our own experiences in life.

Your version is as valid as mine or anyone else’s, so the following images are not to correct your perception, but to share mine with you.
