Book One of the Geronimo Saga

The Geronimo Solution

A crippled 23rd-century starship is stranded in 1886. Without the help of a disgraced ex-cavalryman, its crew may never survive the American West.

First Officer Tamyra Corbett leads a team down to the Arizona Territory in search of the one resource that can keep the starship Vanguard in the sky. Her timing could hardly be worse: the final days of the Apache Wars.

John “Mac” McAllister, a former cavalryman with a prison record, is panning for gold in the Santa Rita Mountains when an Apache war party falls on a group of very strange strangers. Now Mac has to keep Corbett’s crew alive long enough to save their ship, caught between Geronimo’s renegades and a ruthless Army captain.
